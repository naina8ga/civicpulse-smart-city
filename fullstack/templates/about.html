About CivicPulse

A crowdsourced neighborhood issue reporting and municipal resolution ledger.

Residents log street-level infrastructure problems—potholes, broken streetlights, overflowing waste, or water leaks—with a photo and address. Reports automatically route to the responsible municipal department and remain publicly visible through every status transition until resolved.
